Chart privacy policy

Chart is a personal app by Wil Schweitzer.

  • Chart uses Google sign-in only for Wil's own Google account.
  • It uses the drive.file scope, so it can only see files Chart created or files Wil picks.
  • Audio stays in Wil's Google Drive and his browser.
  • A sign-in helper at chart-auth.schweitzerwil.workers.dev stores an encrypted refresh token only so Wil stays signed in.
  • No data is sold or shared. There are no ads or analytics.
  • To revoke access, visit https://myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • Contact: schweitzerwil@gmail.com
